## Engagement tactics

- Post when your network is active (weekday-style hours in your timezone; avoid late night).
- Open with one line that stands alone; keep it short; end with a specific question.
- Add real visuals: a clip beats a screenshot, and a screenshot beats nothing.
- Reply to every comment within the first hour; comments count toward engagement.
- Ask 5-10 friends and colleagues to comment (a real comment, not just a like).
- Tag collaborators or people you mention.
- Repost or reshare Post 3 on 1 Nov with a short note.
